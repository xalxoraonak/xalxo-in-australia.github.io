Learn the four steps

Start with a pause

Would you share it before checking it?

Imagine opening your class group chat and finding an urgent message: “Tomorrow’s exam has been cancelled. Forward this to everyone!” There is no official notice—just a screenshot. Would you pass it on?

This is the kind of moment our “Verify Before You Share” campaign asks you to notice. Instead of forwarding immediately, pause and look for the original announcement.

A familiar sender is not the same thing as an original source. Ask where the information first came from.

Your checking toolkit

Four steps before your next share

Use these four prompts as a reminder to slow down, investigate, and put a claim in context.

S

Stop

Pause before reacting. What is the claim, and do you know enough about it to share it?

I

Investigate the source

Find out who published the information and what relevant expertise or evidence they provide.

F

Find better coverage

Look beyond the post. Search for independent reporting or relevant official information about the claim.

T

Trace the original

Follow a quote, image, or claim back to its original context. Check what the full source actually says.

Hypothetical example

Back to that exam message

Rather than forwarding the screenshot, open your college’s official notice page or ask the examination office. Look for a dated announcement that names the relevant exam.

If you cannot confirm it, say so: “I haven’t verified this. Please check the official notice before acting on it.” You can leave a claim unshared while you investigate.

Make it a habit

Your before-sharing checklist

  • Read the full story. Does the headline match what the article actually says?
  • Check the date and location. Is the post about the event being discussed now?
  • Look for the original source. Can you find the full announcement, interview, or report?
  • Check the image’s context. Try a reverse-image search and compare where else it appears.
  • Be honest about uncertainty. If you cannot verify the claim, do not present it as confirmed.

The takeaway

Conclusion: Small Pause, Big Difference

A safer digital world starts with the choices we make every day. Before sharing a headline, image, or video, take a moment to question it, check its source, and look for reliable coverage.

You do not have to know everything—you just need to be willing to check. With SafeScroll Foundation’s “Verify Before You Share” campaign, let’s make thoughtful sharing a daily habit.

Pause. Check. Share responsibly. Be part of the solution, not the spread.

Be the person who checks

Start with your next share. Ask where it came from, look for context, and encourage your friends to do the same.
